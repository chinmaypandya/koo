Koo: an AI flat hunter for Facebook groups

Stop scrolling. Appoint Koo. Koo is a free Chrome extension that reads Facebook flat-rental groups for you and keeps only the flats that fit your budget, location and filters.

The struggle

Hundreds of new flat posts a day, thousands a week, new ones every few hours. Reply late and it is already booked. You scroll all day to stay first, then filter every post by hand.

How Koo works

  1. Open a Facebook flat group.
  2. Set your budget, location and other filters.
  3. Press Start. Koo reads every post.
  4. Get only the matches, with phone numbers.

One real run: 146 posts read in 4 minutes, 1 match.

Pricing
